# The request arms on Qwen3-8B-AWQ (S1.2, S1.3, S1.4)

Generated by `scripts/bakeoff_report.py scripts/bakeoff_runs --control qwen3-8b-awq-control`
(`scorecard.md` and `scorecard.json`, both gitignored with the run directories), 2026-09-15.

**What this is, and what it is not.** Five sessions over the 174-case bank
(`709265958412f811`, seed 42), all of them the **same weights** — `Qwen/Qwen3-8B-AWQ` served by
the pinned vLLM 0.28.0. They differ only in what the request asks for. So this answers S1.2, S1.3
and S1.4, and it is **not** S2.4: the first-wave bake-off needs other model families, and none has
been run.

## Is any arm distinguishable from the control?

No. Paired tests over the same cases, Holm-corrected:

| test | n | statistic | p | p (Holm) |

| accuracy `vote_first_choice`, all five | 40 | Cochran's Q = 5.57, df 4 | 0.2341 | 1 |
| accuracy, budget-2048 vs control | 40 | McNemar exact, discordant 3/0 | 0.25 | 1 |
| accuracy, budget-4096 vs control | 40 | McNemar exact, discordant 0/0 | 1 | 1 |
| accuracy, thinking-sampling vs control | 40 | McNemar exact, discordant 4/6 | 0.7539 | 1 |
| accuracy, vote-grammar vs control | 40 | McNemar exact, discordant 0/0 | 1 | 1 |
| validity `vote_cast`, all five | 14 | Cochran's Q = 4.00, df 4 | 0.406 | 1 |

With 40 and 14 cases, that is what this bank can say: no arm is measurably better at voting. The
differences worth acting on are elsewhere — validity, truncation and cost, which are counts of
things that either happened or did not.

## The logprob gate

Only the grammar passes it: 16/16 aligned, against 13/16 for the control and for both budgets. Its
separation is weaker — **+0.652** against the control's **+0.942** — which is what constraining the
decode to a grammar does to the distribution. Worth knowing before adoption, not an objection.

## The noise floor

Re-runs of the same cases: the control reproduces its own output 16/17 = 94.1% [73.0, 99.0] and its
units 83/86 = 96.5% [90.2, 98.8]. The arms were re-run 2 or 3 times each and reproduced everything,
which at that many trials means little (Wilson 95% reaches down to 34%).
