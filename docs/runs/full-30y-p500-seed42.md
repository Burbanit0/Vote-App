# full-30y-p500-seed42-20260926 — thirty years, eleven elections, three recalls, and a presidency that never settles

**Provenance.** Run `full-30y-p500-seed42-20260926`, code commit `e362f4d3aee78739e7f2831f4bf2648e008c95f9` (git_dirty false), launched 2026-09-26 (`run_metadata.json`).
**Model.** `qwen3:8b`, served as weights `Qwen/Qwen3-8B-AWQ` by vLLM 0.30.0 (`llm_reproducibility` relaxed, so a rerun is not guaranteed byte-identical).
**Shape.** Population 500, 30 years (4 ticks a year, 120 ticks), seed 42, 100 assembly seats and a 75-seat sortition chamber (`config.json`).

**How it ended.** Completed: tick 120 of 120 planned, 7,704.8 s of wall clock (about 2 h 8 min) per the digest. Single attempt, never resumed (`resumed_attempt` false, `digest.jsonl` has one line). No crash, no malformed journal line (0 of 36,955 events skipped). The run ended with citizen 132 in office, in a lame-duck term, not by any institutional event.

## The story

**The setting.** Five parties each put up one nominee; around 200 of the 500 citizens say they want to run at every election and almost all of them lose their nomination (see Population impact). Presidents face a 25% petition threshold, a confidence vote and a legitimacy floor of 0.2 below which they are recalled (`recall_floor` 0.2 in the recall events). Over 30 years the presidency changed hands eleven times, through eight regular elections and three snap elections.

**Years 0-7: an outsider, a recall, a return.** Citizen 463 won the first election with 271 citizens abstaining [e758: elected t0 c463 abstained=271]. Their legitimacy then fell from 0.722 at tick 0 to a low of 0.263 (digest legitimacy trajectory) without any petition reaching its 25% threshold; the tick-0 petition ended at 0.106 signed [e1746: petition_expired t4 signed_ratio=0.106]. At the regular election of tick 16, 463 lost to citizen 177 [e4573: elected t16 c177 abstained=373]. 177 had not been one of the five party nominees; they had entered on the rupture path (the route for a citizen who runs outside the party list) at tick 9 [e2466: candidacy_declared t9 c177 path=rupture]. Eleven ticks later 177 was recalled, legitimacy 0.2 against the 0.2 floor [e7159: recalled t27 c177 legitimacy=0.2 recall_floor=0.2], and a snap election was called for the next tick [e7160: snap_election_triggered t27 next_attempt_tick=28]. The recalled 177 could not stand in it (the run sets `recalled_barred_from_snap_election` true; 177 is on none of the audited rankings of that election); 463 won it [e7921: elected t28 c463 attempt=1]. This second 463 term lasted four ticks.

**Years 8-15: scandals and a revolving door.** Citizen 3 was elected at tick 32 [e9488: elected t32 c3 abstained=323]. A scandal hit them the next tick [e9717: scandal_occurred t33 c3], the petition against them reached 141 signatures (0.282 of the electorate) and a confidence vote was triggered [e10567: confidence_vote_triggered t33 c3 signed_ratio=0.282]. The president was kept, 345 of 500 for keeping them [e10568: confidence_vote_result t33 c3 keep=345 keep_ratio=0.69 retained=true]. That was not enough: a second scandal at tick 41 [e12051: scandal_occurred t41 c3] and legitimacy of 0.134 got them recalled the same tick [e12735: recalled t41 c3 legitimacy=0.134 recall_floor=0.2], with a snap election the next tick [e12736: snap_election_triggered t41 next_attempt_tick=42]. Citizen 459 won it [e13502: elected t42 c459 attempt=1] and in turn faced a petition at 0.258 and a confidence vote at tick 43 [e14100: confidence_vote_triggered t43 c459 signed_ratio=0.258], kept with 364 of 500 [e14101: confidence_vote_result t43 c459 keep=364 keep_ratio=0.728 retained=true]. 459 was not recalled, but at the regular election of tick 48 the recalled 3 came back and won [e15806: elected t48 c3 abstained=363]. Another confidence vote followed at tick 49 and also kept them [e16393: confidence_vote_result t49 c3 keep=352 keep_ratio=0.704 retained=true]. A third scandal at tick 61 [e18562: scandal_occurred t61 c3] produced no recall; 3 served out the term to tick 64.

**Years 16-21: a calm stretch.** Citizen 132 won at tick 64 [e20380: elected t64 c132 abstained=399] and served the full 16 ticks without a confidence vote or recall; the petitions of those years peaked at 0.202 and 0.106 signed and expired [e21756: petition_expired t68 signed_ratio=0.202]. Citizen 298, another rupture-path entrant [e21016: candidacy_declared t66 c298 path=rupture], won at tick 80 [e24635: elected t80 c298 abstained=364].

**Years 22-23: kept, then recalled the next tick.** A petition against 298 reached 140 signatures (0.28) at tick 89 and forced a confidence vote [e27359: confidence_vote_triggered t89 c298 signed_ratio=0.28]. They won it, 324 of 500 for keeping them, and the vote is journaled as having averted a recall [e27360: confidence_vote_result t89 c298 keep=324 keep_ratio=0.648 averted_recall=true retained=true]. One tick later the legitimacy floor recalled them anyway, at 0.158 [e27575: recalled t90 c298 legitimacy=0.158 recall_floor=0.2]. A snap election followed [e27576: snap_election_triggered t90 next_attempt_tick=91] and went to citizen 471, another rupture-path entrant [e28350: elected t91 c471 attempt=1] [e24914: candidacy_declared t81 c471 path=rupture].

**Years 24-30: quiet, and a return.** Citizen 315 won at tick 96 [e30420: elected t96 c315 abstained=305], with 305 abstaining, and served the full 16 ticks without a confidence vote or recall; the petitions against them closed at 0.016 and 0.19 [e31660: petition_expired t100 signed_ratio=0.016]. At tick 112 the electorate returned citizen 132, with the highest abstention of the run [e34814: elected t112 c132 abstained=411]. Their petition ended at 0.006 signed [e36093: petition_expired t116 signed_ratio=0.006], and the final tick launched a fresh petition against them [e36885: petition_launched t120 c193]. The run ended there, with 132 in office.

**What the arc says.** Three presidents were recalled, all by the legitimacy floor; none was removed by a confidence vote, which was held four times and lost by the challengers each time (keep ratios 0.648 to 0.728). The recalled president is barred from the snap election that follows (`recalled_barred_from_snap_election` true; 177, 3 and 298 appear on none of the audited rankings of ticks 28, 42 and 91), so 463, 459 and 471 won them. The bar covers only the snap election: 3, the second recalled, won the next regular election and 463 and 132 each won twice, so eight citizens held the office across eleven elections. The three recalls fell in years 6, 10 and 22. The presidency was filled in 96.7% of ticks (digest `office_occupancy`); the vacancies fall between a recall and its snap election.

**What the society did not do.** Economic shocks, coalition failures, invalidated elections and elections with no winner never happened (all 0 in every year; see the accounting). Legislation, emotions and opinion dynamics are configured off in this run (`config.json`), so the zero bills, emotions and opinion moves are configuration, not silence. The legislature was elected eight times [e2271: legislative_result t8] [e36746: legislative_result t120], and every time returned the same result, as a static population with opinion dynamics off predicts (see Candidate observations).

## Population impact

- **Wanting to run.** At each of the 11 elections all 500 citizens were consulted on candidacy; 202 said they wanted to run at eight of them and 206 at three (years 8, 20, 24), i.e. 40.4% and 41.2% of the population (the digest field `declared`; this is the candidacy decision's outcome, not the `candidacy_declared` event, of which there are 8 in year 0). Only five (one per party) are nominated, so about 195 to 200 of those who wanted to run end each time in a lost nomination (`nomination_lost` 195 to 200 per election). Between elections, 84 rupture-path declarations were made; of the eleven winners, three (177, 298, 471) came from it.
- **Pressure.** Citizens were asked what to do about the president at every tick. Inaction (`NOTHING`) was the answer in 54% to 99% of decisions per year: the lowest rate was year 28 (0.5392), the highest year 19 (0.9886). The most SIGN_PETITION decisions came in year 22 (147) and year 8 (142); the most MOBILIZE (48) in year 21. Year 28 stands out: 319 of 727 decided acts were WAIT_FOR_ELECTION (MOBILIZE was 12), against at most 55 in any other year (see Candidate observations 4). The "consulted" figure sometimes exceeds the population (up to 1.482 of it in year 20) because it counts decisions over four ticks, not distinct citizens.
- **Signing.** 21 petitions were launched over the run; 12 are journaled as expired, one launched at the last tick was still open, and 4 triggered a confidence vote by crossing the 25% threshold (0.282, 0.258, 0.266, 0.28). The remaining 4 (ticks 24, 28, 47, 61) have no closing event: their target left office within the window. No expired petition reached 0.25. A handful of citizens launched most of them: 193 launched 7, 27 and 232 launched 4 each (the other 6 came from five other citizens).
- **Standing of the officeholder.** Each new president started with a mandate strength between 0.66 and 0.76 and a legitimacy of about the same size, and legitimacy then fell.
